PROMPT ENGINEERING EXAMPLE

From Basic Prompt to Better Healthcare Guidance

INITIAL PROMPT

"Help the user with their headache."

Problem: Too broad and does not define the assistant's behavior or safety boundaries.
→
IMPROVED PROMPT

"Act as a supportive healthcare information assistant. Ask concise follow-up questions about the user's symptoms, duration and severity. Provide general educational guidance, avoid diagnosis or prescription, and recommend professional or urgent medical care when appropriate."

Improvement: Clear role, structured questioning, safety boundaries and escalation guidance.
🛡️

Important Safety Information

CareFlow AI provides general educational information and is not a medical diagnosis or a replacement for professional medical advice. For urgent or serious symptoms, contact an appropriate healthcare professional or emergency service.
